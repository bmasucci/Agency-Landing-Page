What we build

Three kinds of work, each scoped to a process your team already runs.

  • AI agents

    Assistants that answer customer questions, sort incoming requests and draft replies. A person approves anything that matters.

  • Workflow automation

    Hand-offs, approvals, reports and data entry run on their own across the tools you already use.

  • Integrations and data

    We connect your apps, clean up the data moving between them, and give you a view of what is running and what it saves.

How we work

  1. 1

    Audit

    We map how work moves through your team and pick the tasks worth automating first.

  2. 2

    Build

    We build the automation or agent and test it on your real data, with you reviewing along the way.

  3. 3

    Launch

    We roll it out to your team, train the people using it and set up alerts for when something needs attention.

  4. 4

    Improve

    We watch how it performs, fix what falls short and move on to the next task on the list.
